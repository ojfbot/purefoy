## 💡 Roger's Philosophy on Film

From the discussions, Roger emphasizes:
- Films that "illustrate what power film can have"
- Visual storytelling over pure style
- Cinematography that serves the story
- International cinema and diverse perspectives
- Films with emotional depth and meaning

**For Cinematography Study:**
